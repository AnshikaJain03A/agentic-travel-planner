# India Travel FAQ and General Tips

## Currency and Payments
The currency is the Indian Rupee (INR). UPI and cards are widely accepted in
cities, but carry cash for small towns, markets, autos and beach shacks.

## Transport
- Domestic flights connect all major cities and are the fastest option.
- Trains are economical and scenic; book early on IRCTC for sleeper/AC classes.
- For local travel use app cabs (Ola/Uber), autos, or rented scooters/cars.
- Always agree on auto/taxi fares or insist on the meter before starting.

## Accommodation by Travel Style
- Budget: hostels, guesthouses and homestays (INR 800-2500/night).
- Family: serviced apartments and mid-range hotels with safety and space.
- Luxury: 4-5 star resorts and boutique heritage properties.
- Adventure: eco-camps, treehouses and locations close to activity hubs.
- Solo: well-reviewed hostels in safe, central neighbourhoods.

## Health and Safety
- Drink bottled or filtered water; avoid raw salads from uncertain sources.
- Carry basic medication, sunscreen and mosquito repellent.
- Keep digital and physical copies of ID and travel documents.
- Share your itinerary with someone you trust.
- All-India emergency number: 112. Ambulance: 108. Police: 100.

## Etiquette
- Dress modestly at religious sites; remove shoes before entering temples.
- Ask before photographing people, ceremonies or military/airport areas.
- Bargaining is expected in markets but not in fixed-price shops.

## Weather Overview
India has three broad seasons: winter (Nov-Feb, best for most regions), summer
(Mar-Jun, hot), and monsoon (Jun-Sep, heavy rain). Hill stations are pleasant in
summer; coastal and desert regions are best in winter.
