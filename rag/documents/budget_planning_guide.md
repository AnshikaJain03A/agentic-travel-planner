# Travel Budget Planning Guide (India)

A practical framework for estimating trip costs in INR. Figures are typical
per-person, per-day ranges and should be scaled by destination and season.

## Transport (round trip from departure city)
- Budget: trains / buses, INR 1,500-4,000 per person.
- Mid-range: economy flights, INR 4,000-9,000 per person.
- Luxury: full-fare / premium flights, INR 9,000-20,000 per person.
- Add local transport: INR 300-800 per day (scooter, autos, app cabs).

## Accommodation (per night, per room for two)
- Budget: INR 800-2,500 (hostels, guesthouses).
- Mid-range / family: INR 2,500-6,000 (3-star hotels, apartments).
- Luxury: INR 8,000-30,000+ (resorts, boutique hotels).

## Food (per person, per day)
- Budget: INR 500-900 (local eateries, street food, thalis).
- Mid-range: INR 900-1,800 (cafes, sit-down restaurants).
- Luxury: INR 2,000-5,000 (fine dining, resort restaurants).

## Activities (per person, per day)
- Sightseeing and entry tickets: INR 200-800.
- Water sports / adventure: INR 1,000-3,000 per activity.
- Guided tours and experiences: INR 800-2,500.

## Rule of Thumb Allocation
A balanced budget often splits roughly as:
- Transport: 25-35%
- Accommodation: 25-35%
- Food: 15-25%
- Activities: 10-20%
- Miscellaneous / buffer: 10% (always keep a contingency).

## Money-Saving Tips
- Travel in shoulder season for cheaper flights and stays.
- Book flights and trains early; prices rise close to the date.
- Choose accommodation with breakfast included.
- Mix paid attractions with free beaches, parks, markets and walks.
- Use public transport or shared rides over private cabs where safe.
